FAQ

WhatsApp Automation — your questions, answered

Is this the official WhatsApp API or a grey-market tool?

Official, always. We set up the genuine WhatsApp Business API through Meta-approved providers — with proper verification and opt-in flows. Unofficial tools risk your number being permanently banned, taking your customer relationships with it.

Can we send marketing broadcasts on WhatsApp?

Yes — to customers who have opted in, using Meta-approved message templates. Done properly, broadcasts see 98% open rates and 45–60% click rates. We handle template approval and compliance so campaigns run smoothly.

What happens when the AI can't handle a conversation?

It transfers seamlessly to your team with full conversation context, and the customer never has to repeat themselves. You define exactly which situations go to humans — pricing negotiations, complaints, VIP clients — and the AI respects those rules.

Can multiple team members use one WhatsApp number?

Yes — that's one of the API's biggest advantages. Your whole team works from a shared inbox with conversation assignment, internal notes and full history. No more 'the customer messaged Dave's phone and Dave is on holiday'.

How much does WhatsApp automation cost?

Setup projects typically start around $1,200 including API onboarding, bot build and integrations. Meta charges small per-conversation fees (roughly $0.005–$0.15 depending on country and type), which we help you optimise. Most clients see the system pay for itself within the first month.

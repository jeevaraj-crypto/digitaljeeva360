FAQ

Workflow Automation — your questions, answered

Which is better — n8n, Zapier or Make?

It depends on your needs. Zapier is fastest to deploy, Make offers strong visual complexity at lower cost, and n8n gives unlimited flexibility with self-hosting for sensitive data. We're experts in all three and recommend based on your volume, budget and data-privacy requirements — not on what's fashionable.

What processes should we automate first?

The sweet spot is high-frequency, rule-based tasks: lead capture and routing, appointment reminders, invoice follow-ups, data entry and reporting. Our audit ranks every opportunity by hours saved versus build effort, so you always start with the fastest payback.

What if an automation breaks?

Every workflow we build includes error handling and instant alerts — you'll know within minutes, not weeks. Clients on care plans get proactive monitoring where we usually fix issues before anyone notices.

Can automation work with our custom or legacy software?

Usually, yes. If it has an API, webhook support, email output or even a database we can read, we can integrate it. We've connected 20-year-old ERP systems to modern AI tooling.

How is this different from hiring a virtual assistant?

A VA costs $1,500–$3,000 every month, works set hours, and takes knowledge with them when they leave. Automation is a one-time build that works 24/7, never resigns, and gets cheaper per task the more you grow. Many clients use both — automation for volume, humans for judgement.

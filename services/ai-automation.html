FAQ

AI Automation — your questions, answered

How long does it take to build an AI automation system?

Most first-phase systems go live in 2–4 weeks. Simple automations (lead capture, follow-up sequences) can be live within days, while complex multi-department systems roll out in phases over 6–8 weeks — you start seeing time savings from the very first phase.

Will AI automation work with the tools we already use?

Almost certainly. We integrate with 5,000+ platforms through n8n, Make and Zapier — including every major CRM, email platform, calendar, e-commerce system and accounting tool. If your software has an API, we can connect it.

What does AI automation cost?

Projects typically start around $1,500 for a focused single-workflow build, with comprehensive business systems ranging from $5,000–$25,000. Every proposal includes a projected ROI, and most clients recover their investment within 60–90 days.

Will automation feel robotic to my customers?

No — that's the difference between cheap bots and well-engineered AI. We train systems on your brand voice, real conversations and FAQs, with seamless human handoff for complex situations. Most customers can't tell the difference, and they love the instant response.

Do I need technical knowledge to manage the system?

None at all. We deliver everything with plain-English documentation, video walkthroughs and a simple dashboard. You can also opt for a monthly care plan where we monitor, maintain and improve everything for you.

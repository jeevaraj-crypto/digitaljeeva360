FAQ

Email Automation — your questions, answered

Which email platform do you recommend?

For e-commerce, Klaviyo is usually the strongest choice. For B2B and service businesses, ActiveCampaign or HubSpot. Already on another platform? We work with what you have — strategy and copy matter far more than the logo on the software.

How many emails is too many?

Frequency matters less than relevance. A behaviour-triggered email arriving at the perfect moment is welcomed; a random blast is ignored. Our flows self-regulate — subscribers only receive what's relevant to where they are in their journey, which keeps unsubscribes low.

Do you write the emails or do we?

We write everything — subject lines, body copy and calls to action — using proven direct-response frameworks in your brand voice. You review and approve before anything goes live.

How quickly will we see revenue from email flows?

Cart abandonment and welcome flows typically produce revenue within the first week of going live. Full programmes usually reach their stride within 60 days as data accumulates and we optimise.

Will this hurt our sender reputation?

The opposite. We implement proper authentication, sunset unengaged contacts and warm up sending domains correctly — most clients see deliverability improve significantly once we take over.

FAQ

SEO & Organic Growth — your questions, answered

How long does SEO take to show results?

Meaningful movement typically starts at 60–90 days, with significant results at 6–9 months. SEO is slower than ads but compounds: the traffic you earn keeps flowing without ongoing spend. We front-load quick wins — technical fixes and low-competition keywords — so you see progress early.

What are GEO and AEO, and do I need them?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets your brand cited in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) structures your content to be the direct answer to questions. As more searches end in AI answers instead of blue links, brands optimised for citation capture disproportionate visibility — early movers are winning big right now.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No honest SEO agency guarantees specific rankings — Google's algorithm isn't for sale, and anyone promising positions is planning to show you rankings for worthless keywords. We do commit to transparent reporting, proven methodology and measurable growth in qualified organic traffic and leads.

We tried SEO before and saw nothing. Why would this be different?

Usually because previous work was activity without strategy — generic blogs, directory links, jargon reports. Our approach starts from revenue: which searches indicate buying intent, what will it take to win them, and what is each ranking worth? Everything we do maps to that.

How much do SEO services cost?

Retainers start around $800/month for focused local campaigns, with competitive national campaigns typically $1,500–$4,000/month. After the audit you'll get a clear recommendation with expected outcomes — and we'll tell you honestly if SEO isn't your best next investment.

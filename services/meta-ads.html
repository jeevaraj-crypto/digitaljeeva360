FAQ

Meta Ads — your questions, answered

Do Facebook ads still work after the iOS privacy changes?

Yes — for accounts built for the new era. The changes hurt advertisers relying on granular interest targeting and browser-only tracking. Modern performance comes from strong creative (the real targeting now), Conversions API signals and broad audiences the algorithm can optimise. Our clients' results prove the channel is very much alive.

What makes ad creative 'good' on Meta?

The first two seconds. Winning ads hook instantly (a bold claim, a pattern interrupt, a relatable problem), feel native to the feed rather than corporate, and sell through story or demonstration. We test hooks systematically because nobody — including us — can reliably predict winners without data.

How much should we spend on Meta Ads?

E-commerce brands should start at $3,000+/month to feed the algorithm enough conversion events; lead generation can start around $1,500/month. Under those levels, learning phases drag and results stay noisy. We'll give you an honest minimum for your specific situation.

Do you produce the ad creative or do we?

We handle it — concepts, copy, static design and video editing, including briefing UGC creators when authentic content fits the brand. You approve everything, and we can also amplify content you already produce.

Instagram or Facebook — where will my ads show?

Both, automatically optimised by placement performance. Meta's system delivers your ads wherever your buyers respond best across Facebook, Instagram, Reels, Stories and Messenger — we design creative formats to look native in every placement.

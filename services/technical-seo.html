FAQ

Technical SEO — your questions, answered

What does a technical SEO audit include?

A full crawl of your site, Search Console and log analysis, Core Web Vitals assessment on real-user data, indexation and canonical review, schema audit, architecture and internal-link analysis, plus mobile and rendering checks. You get a prioritised, plain-English action plan — not a 90-page automated export.

How do I know if technical issues are hurting my site?

Common symptoms: pages that never appear in Google, rankings stuck despite good content, traffic that dropped after a redesign, or Search Console warnings you're unsure about. The audit turns those mysteries into a concrete diagnosis with fixes.

What are Core Web Vitals and do they really affect rankings?

They're Google's user-experience metrics: LCP (loading), INP (interactivity) and CLS (visual stability). They're a confirmed ranking factor — and more importantly, they measure the experience that decides whether visitors stay or bounce. Improving them lifts both rankings and conversions.

Can you implement fixes, or just tell us what's wrong?

Both. We implement directly on most platforms (WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, custom builds), or deliver developer-ready tickets with exact specifications for your team. Either way, we validate every fix after deployment.

We're planning a redesign — when should technical SEO get involved?

Before a single page is built. Migrations done without SEO planning routinely lose 30–70% of organic traffic through broken redirects and lost page equity. Involved early, we make redesigns a ranking opportunity instead of a catastrophe.

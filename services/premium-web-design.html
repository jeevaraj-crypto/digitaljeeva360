FAQ

Premium Website Development — your questions, answered

How much does a premium website cost?

Business websites typically range from $2,000–$8,000 depending on size and functionality; e-commerce and complex builds from $6,000–$20,000. Every quote is fixed-price after a scoping call — no hourly billing surprises. Compare that with what a website that actually converts adds to revenue over three years, and it's one of the highest-ROI investments available.

How long does a website project take?

Landing pages: 1–2 weeks. Business websites: 3–6 weeks. E-commerce and larger builds: 6–10 weeks. You'll get a clear timeline with milestones before we start, and we hit them.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes — we build on platforms with clean, simple editing (WordPress, Webflow or a headless CMS), and provide video training. Most clients handle day-to-day updates in minutes, with us on call for anything bigger.

Do you redesign existing websites or only build new ones?

Both. Redesigns are actually our specialty — we preserve your SEO equity (rankings, URLs, backlinks) with proper redirects and migration planning, while transforming design and conversion performance. Done wrong, redesigns destroy rankings; we've made sure that never happens.

What makes your websites different from a cheap template site?

Three things: custom design around your brand rather than a theme thousands of sites share; engineering for speed and SEO instead of plugin-bloat; and conversion strategy — every page is designed to move visitors toward an enquiry. A template site is an expense; ours is an asset that pays for itself.

FAQ

AI & Growth Consulting — your questions, answered

How is consulting different from your done-for-you services?

Consulting gives you the strategy and roadmap; done-for-you services execute it. Many clients start with a consulting engagement to get clarity, then have us build the highest-priority items. Others take the roadmap to their internal team. Both paths work — the roadmap is yours either way.

What does a consulting engagement cost?

A comprehensive audit and roadmap engagement typically runs $2,000–$5,000 depending on business complexity. Ongoing fractional advisory starts around $1,000/month. Compare that with the cost of six months spent building the wrong things.

We're not technical. Will the recommendations be over our heads?

No — translating technology into business language is precisely the job. Every recommendation is explained in terms of hours saved, revenue added and payback period, with implementation plans your team can actually follow.

Can you review tools and proposals we've already received?

Yes, and it often saves clients thousands. We regularly review vendor proposals, quotes and tool subscriptions — telling you what's fairly priced, what's overkill and what's missing. Vendor-neutral means we have no incentive but your outcome.

How quickly can we start?

Discovery calls are usually available within the week, and a full audit takes 2–3 weeks from kickoff. If you have an urgent decision — a proposal deadline or a system purchase — ask about a rapid advisory session.

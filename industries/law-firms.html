FAQ

Law Firms — your questions, answered

Is AI intake appropriate for confidential legal matters?

The AI handles logistics and screening — practice area, timeline, contact details, appointment booking — not legal advice or privileged discussion. Sensitive matters route to your team by rules you define, and everything is logged for your records. Most firms find it more consistent and more discreet than a rushed front desk.

Can it screen out cases we don't take?

Yes — that's one of its biggest wins. Wrong practice area, outside your jurisdiction, below your case threshold: the AI politely declines or refers out, based entirely on your criteria. Your attorneys' calendars fill with consultations worth their time.

We already use a legal answering service. Why switch?

Answering services take messages; the AI resolves calls. It answers instantly with zero hold time, actually qualifies the case, books the consultation and writes structured intake into your system — at a flat monthly rate instead of per-minute billing that punishes you for being busy.

How competitive is legal SEO in Dallas, honestly?

Very — personal injury especially, where the biggest firms spend millions. But most DFW firms compete in specific practice areas and suburbs where focused local SEO absolutely wins: 'family lawyer Frisco' is a different battle than 'car accident lawyer Dallas'.

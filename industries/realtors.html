FAQ

Realtors — your questions, answered

Will automated follow-up feel impersonal to my clients?

Done right, it feels more personal, not less — because nobody gets forgotten. Messages are written in your voice, reference their actual criteria, and you're alerted the moment someone shows buying signals so the human conversations happen at exactly the right time.

Does this work with my CRM (Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, etc.)?

Yes — we build on top of what you use. Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, LionDesk, Sierra and most major real estate CRMs integrate cleanly. If your current setup is a spreadsheet, we'll get you onto a proper pipeline as part of the build.

I'm a solo agent, not a team. Is this overkill?

Solo agents benefit most — you're the one losing leads every time you're in a showing. The system acts as your inside sales assistant at a fraction of the cost of hiring one, and it scales with you if you build a team later.

What does this cost compared to buying more leads?

Most agents don't have a lead problem — they have a conversion problem. Recovering the leads you already pay for is dramatically cheaper than buying more. Typical setups run $1,500–$4,000 with modest monthly costs; one extra closing pays for years of it.

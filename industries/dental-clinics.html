Is the AI receptionist appropriate for a medical setting?

Yes — it's built for exactly this. It handles scheduling, insurance questions and routine information, while anything clinical or urgent routes instantly to your team or on-call dentist. We configure it around your protocols, and it never gives medical advice.

Can it integrate with our practice management software?

We integrate with most major dental PMS platforms directly or through their scheduling links, and where direct integration isn't possible, the AI books into a synced calendar your front desk works from. We'll confirm your exact setup on the strategy call.

How do you get us more Google reviews?

Automatically and compliantly: after each completed visit, happy patients get a well-timed text with a direct review link. Steady review velocity — not bought reviews — is what moves you up the map pack in competitive DFW suburbs.

How quickly can a dental practice see results?

The AI receptionist starts recovering missed calls from day one, and reminder automation cuts no-shows within the first month. Map-pack movement in DFW typically takes 60–120 days depending on your suburb's competition. Ads produce booked consults within weeks once tracking is in place.

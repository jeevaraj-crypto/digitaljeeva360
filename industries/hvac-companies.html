FAQ

HVAC Companies — your questions, answered

Can the AI actually dispatch emergency calls?

It answers, triages by your rules — no cooling with elderly residents or a gas smell gets flagged urgent — books the job into your schedule, and immediately alerts your on-call tech by call or text with the full details. You define what counts as an emergency; it executes flawlessly at 3 am.

Does it integrate with ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro / Jobber?

Yes — we integrate with the major field-service platforms for scheduling, customer records and job creation. Your dispatchers see AI-booked jobs exactly like any other booking, with cleaner notes than most humans leave.

How fast can we be ready for peak season?

The AI receptionist and missed-call text-back go live in 1–2 weeks. Ads take 2–4 weeks to tune. Map-pack SEO is the long game — 60–120 days for meaningful movement — which is why the smart time to start is before the heat wave, not during it.

We've been burned by HVAC lead-gen companies. How is this different?

Shared-lead platforms sell the same homeowner to five contractors and make you race to the phone. Everything we build is yours alone: your rankings, your ads, your AI answering your number. No shared leads, no per-lead fees, and you own every asset if we ever part ways.

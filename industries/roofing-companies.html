FAQ

Roofing Companies — your questions, answered

Can the AI really handle a post-storm call surge?

That's exactly the scenario it wins hardest. It answers unlimited simultaneous calls — no hold queue, no voicemail — books inspections, captures addresses and damage details, and hands your team a clean, prioritised list. The 72 hours that used to overwhelm you become your biggest booking window.

Can it answer insurance claim questions?

It handles the general process questions — how claims work, what to document, typical timelines — using your approved answers, and it never promises coverage outcomes. Its real job is converting the question into a booked inspection, where your team does the real assessment.

How do we compete with storm chasers underpricing us?

You don't win on price — you win on trust and speed. Hundreds of verified local reviews, a professional website with DFW project photos, instant response to every enquiry, and follow-up that outlasts their two-week blitz. Homeowners choose the local company that answers — when that's visibly you, the price conversation changes.

What happens to all this between storms?

That's when the system earns its keep: SEO keeps retail replacement and repair leads flowing, follow-up automation works your estimate backlog, review momentum compounds, and your customer list gets nurtured for referrals and maintenance. Storm revenue becomes the bonus, not the business model.

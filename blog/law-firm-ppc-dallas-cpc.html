Surviving $100 Clicks: A Google Ads Playbook for Dallas Law Firms

Legal keywords in Dallas cost $50-$150 per click. How smart firms make PPC profitable: tight match types, call tracking, instant intake and practice-area landing pages.

J
JeevaFounder, Digital Jeeva360 — AI-Powered Digital Marketer & Automation Specialist

Legal advertising in Dallas is played at the most expensive table in Google's casino. "Car accident lawyer Dallas" runs $80–150 a click. "DWI attorney" isn't far behind. Even family law clicks routinely cost $40–70. At these prices, the difference between a disciplined account and a sloppy one isn't a few percentage points — it's whether marketing produces cases or just invoices.

Here's the playbook we run for DFW firms, in the order that matters.

1. Fix intake before spending a dollar

The most common catastrophe in legal PPC has nothing to do with the ads: the firm pays $120 for a click, the visitor calls, and the phone rings four times before voicemail. At Dallas CPCs, every unanswered call is a three-figure bill for nothing. Instant, 24/7 intake isn't an optimization — it's the prerequisite. Until every ad-driven call gets answered in seconds, more budget just buys more waste.

2. Exact match or bust

Broad match with $100 clicks is a donation to Google. Disciplined legal accounts run exact and tight phrase match on intent-heavy terms ("hire", "attorney near me", "lawyer for X"), backed by an aggressive negative list: "free", "salary", "how to become", "pro bono", every law school in Texas. We typically find 25–40% of a self-managed legal account's spend going to searches that could never become cases.

3. One practice area, one landing page

Sending a custody-search click to your homepage — where they must navigate past your PI banner and your firm history — burns money. Each campaign gets a dedicated page: the specific problem, your specific experience with it, results where bar rules permit, attorney faces, and one action (call or book). Message match lifts conversion and drops your cost per click through better Quality Scores. Details on our landing pages service.

4. Track calls like the assets they are

Most legal conversions are phone calls, not form fills. Without call tracking wired into the account, Google optimizes toward form-fillers — a minority — while your best channel stays invisible. Dynamic number insertion, call recording (with proper disclosure) and marking qualified calls as conversions teaches the algorithm to find callers who become clients, not just callers.

5. Match spend to case economics

A $6,000 month that signs two $15k family-law matters is a great month. The same spend "getting your name out" is a paralegal's salary set on fire.

Work backwards: average case value × close rate tells you your maximum affordable cost per signed case, which sets your cost-per-lead ceiling, which dictates budget. If the numbers can't work in your practice area, we'll say so — sometimes local SEO is the better war to fund first.
